# Policy Memo — Awaab's Law Phase 2 Readiness
> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT** — fictional property, occupants, organisations and data; created for the Casey Method reference implementation. Any resemblance to real properties, firms or persons is coincidental.

Author: Head of Assets · Date: 2026-06-20

From 30 November 2026, Phase 2 extends statutory duties beyond damp and mould to a wider set of HHSRS hazards, with fixed timescales: emergency hazards made safe within 24 hours; significant hazards investigated within 10 working days, with a written summary to the resident within 3 working days of the investigation concluding.

Operational implications:
1. Repair records must evidence response times end-to-end (report → triage → attendance → completion). Data gaps are a compliance exposure, not just an ops issue.
2. Any reported damp or mould is treated as requiring assessment regardless of how the resident describes it. Tenant phrasing does not downgrade a hazard.
3. Occupant vulnerability (children, elderly, respiratory conditions) raises priority and must be visible to triage staff and any automated triage assistance.
4. Sensor telemetry (humidity, damp risk scoring) is admissible supporting evidence of proactive monitoring; retention period 6 years.
